Sound the horn... okay, we do not have one yet, but 0.2.3 is here!

After the massive 0.2.2 update, we decided to take a short break from big features and focus on what matters just as much: stability, polish, and bug fixes. But as usual, even a "small" update ended up being surprisingly packed.

Dropping items is now safer — they no longer fly right back into your hands like a boomerang. The interface is more convenient, the world feels more believable, torches are more useful, world generation is more interesting, and caves are now much darker and more atmospheric.

Enjoy the game!

---

## Changes and Improvements

### Gameplay

* Fixed item dropping: dropped items are no longer picked up instantly because of a weak throw impulse
* Added a small chance for sticks to drop from leaves
* Fixed furnace breaking: if you break a furnace while something is smelting, its contents now drop together with the furnace
* Re-entering a world now correctly returns the player to the last saved position instead of the original spawn point

---

### Interface and Controls

* Added mouse support in the world selection menu
* Added mouse support in the world creation menu
* Interface scale can now be changed in settings
* Hotbar item count text is now more readable

---

### World and Atmosphere

* Fixed the visual appearance of torches
* Torches no longer stop emitting light at a distance
* Torches can now be placed on walls
* Caves are now significantly darker
* Light now gradually enters through openings to the outside
* Improved underwater visibility: underwater fog and water transparency are now easier to read
* Fixed water rendering so flowing water looks cleaner and better reflects fill levels
* Water now fills terrain shapes more naturally
* Improved how water behaves in depressions and natural basins

---

### World Generation

* Improved ocean generation
* Added rivers
* Lake generation is now more natural
* Removed tiny random puddles
* Smoothed harsh terrain transitions between biomes
* Fixed overly sharp walls between plains and deserts
* Fixed generation on large and random seeds: worlds no longer collapse into overly flat fields
* Random seed now truly creates different worlds
* Deserts now have fewer trees
* Added dry trees to deserts

---

### Technical Changes

* Fixed biome display in debug mode — it now matches the visible surface and climate more accurately
* High rocky ridges are now more correctly identified as mountains in debug mode
* Improved the overall logic for smoothing terrain and biome transitions
* Improved world loading logic around the player's saved position

---

### General

* Various stability improvements and minor bug fixes

---

## Developer Note

This update lays the groundwork for the next major step — **Survival Update (0.3.0)**. We are continuing to expand progression, world systems, and gameplay mechanics so CubeOS can grow into a more complete game.
